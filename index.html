💌 A Letter For You

Jessica ❤️

If you are reading this, it means I finally put into words something that cannot really fit inside words.

Nine months ago, I didn’t know how much one person could change my life. I didn’t know that a single connection could shift the way I think, the way I breathe, the way I see every single day. And then you came in, and slowly, without noise, without force, you became the center of my world.

You are not just someone I love. You are the place my mind goes when everything is loud. You are the calm in every storm I don’t talk about. You are the reason ordinary days suddenly feel like something worth remembering.

There are things I never say out loud, not because they aren’t real, but because they are too big for normal conversation. Like how I catch myself smiling randomly just thinking about you. Or how every future plan somehow always includes you in it, even when I don’t notice it happening.

These past 9 months weren’t perfect, nothing real ever is, but they were ours. And that makes them more valuable than anything perfect could ever be. Every laugh, every small moment, every conversation that went too long at night all of it built something I don’t take for granted.

You make me want to be better without even trying. You make me see things differently. You make me care more, feel more, and believe more in things I didn’t believe in before.

Sometimes I think about how strange it is that out of everyone in the world, it’s you. It’s you I get to talk to, you I get to care about, you I get to miss when you’re not around. And honestly, I don’t question it anymore I just appreciate it.

If I could describe what you are to me, I would say you are my peace and my chaos at the same time. You are my favorite notification, my favorite thought, my favorite silence.

I don’t know what the future holds, but I know what I want it to hold. I want more of this. More memories. More random conversations. More moments where we look back and realize how far we’ve come.

And today, on your birthday, I just want you to feel how important you are. Not just in words, but in everything behind them.

Happy Birthday Jessica ❤️

I hope life treats you gently. I hope you get everything you dream about. And I hope, in some way, I get to be part of that journey with you.

You mean more to me than I could ever properly explain.

And I love you not just in moments, not just in words, but in something consistent that stays even when nothing else does.

Forever yours.
